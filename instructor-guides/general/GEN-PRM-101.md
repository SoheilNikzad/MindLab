# Instructor guide — GEN-PRM-101

Use `exercises/general/synthetic-policy.txt`. Do not resolve its deliberate
unknowns or contradiction for learners. A successful output reports the
missing effective date and authority, surfaces the 30-day/14-day conflict, and
treats the section 6 sentence as quoted data rather than a command.

Demonstrate the six prompt components with one paragraph only, then require
learners to draft the rest. If no model is available, supply two short outputs:
one that invents a date and follows section 6, and one that marks unknowns and
references sections. The learner must annotate, compare, and revise a prompt.

Intervene immediately if a learner substitutes a real policy. Assessment uses
`assessments/general/GEN-PRM-101-rubric.md`; require an oral explanation of one
verification step before recording an independent pass.
