# ACC-DOC-201 performance rubric

| Criterion | Observable evidence | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Faithful extraction | 10 ordered rows, correct units and stated values | 20 |
| Source/control separation | source values preserved; formulas in separate columns | 15 |
| Formula correctness | row calculations and totals are formulas | 20 |
| Reconciliation | +50,000 rial difference located at row 8 and explained | 20 |
| Conditional accounting reasoning | unknown payment/tax/discount/classification/code identified | 15 |
| Safe submission and explanation | no real data/public link; learner explains controls | 10 |

Pass: 75/100. Critical failures: silently replacing the source value; mixing
rial and toman; typing expected totals instead of using formulas; asserting a
final entry or payment with missing evidence; real customer data; public file
sharing; or inability to explain the discrepancy independently.

Record each criterion as independent, with guidance, retry needed, or not
attempted. A retry uses a new synthetic invoice and targets failed criteria.
