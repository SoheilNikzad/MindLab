# Privacy and publication boundary

- Google Drive source files are read-only inputs; do not copy them into the
  public repository.
- Do not publish names, contact fields, banking fields, signatures, learner
  artifacts, access links, or account identifiers found in administrative
  forms.
- Public exercises use synthetic data and say so inside the dataset.
- Do not represent MindLab as approved, endorsed, or affiliated with a named
  university or Microsoft without written authorization.
- A private source may be cited by neutral title and version in the source map;
  do not publish its private URL or internal access metadata.
- Publication review must inspect the Git diff, repository visibility, large
  files, `.env`/secret patterns, and link-sharing assumptions.
